## 2. The operational values, named once

- **`CORPUS_READ_LIMIT = 100`** — `limit`'s default and its cap on `list_corpus` and `list_trajectories` (design A6 :1095; plan
  UI-2 :174). The cursor is base64url of the last entry's sort key; one decoder validates each tool's own key tuple, so a cursor
  one read issued is a schema rejection on the other. `search_corpus` has no cursor (§6.1 :248).
- **The order and its tie-break**, declared, never argued: a diff sits at its `after`; at one instant a CAPTURE before a DIFF, then
  the page's url in code units, then a diff's `before` ascending (the wider pair first, as `loadDiffs` orders), then the record's
  name. Trajectories by the day the claim LEFT, latest first, then the page's url, then the pattern.
- **`since` / `until`** are days, `YYYY-MM-DD`, inclusive at both ends, compared as fourteen digits after the hyphens are removed;
  a capture is placed by its timestamp, a diff by its `after`, a trajectory by the day it left. `INVALID_RANGE` only for
  `since > until`; a malformed day is the schema's refusal.
- **The `pages` facet** is the scope's, never the call's: `first` / `last` are a page's earliest and latest held captures, `entries`
  its captures plus diffs, all unfiltered by `since`, `until`, `page`, `kind` or `cited`, ordered by url; a surveyed page holding
  nothing appears at `all` with `entries: 0` and nulls. The facet carries no title (§28; #479).
- **What `all` costs today:** `pagesInScope` asks `publicPage` per surveyed page (two queries each), and `loadCorpus` loads every
  page of the scope and pages in memory — `limit` bounds the answer, not the work. `search_corpus` at `all` with no range reads
  every held capture's stored text in one query per page; §6.1 :251's measurement is the first reading of this step's exercise
  (43 entries, one page, one call).

## 5. What the sketch predicted that did not happen

- The fixture's stored trajectory rows carried a `patternHash` column and two cases expected its literal; the stored read derives
  a finding's `patternHash` from the presence vector and reads no column, so both cases were unsatisfiable on their world behind
  the loader's red. Found by a REVIEW probe before chunk 3; the expectations now derive the hash the same way.
- `inRange`'s first draft appended the time bounds to the hyphenated day, which is not a fourteen-digit comparison; an `until`
  equal to a capture's own day would have excluded it. Caught at the sketch; a boundary case holds it.
- The world moved from the sketch: PAGE holds the fixture's BETWEEN capture and PAGE_2 three captures, because a two-flip
  trajectory needs three observations over held captures; a third page holds nothing, which the facet's nulls and `undetected`
  need. Eight entries at `all`, four at `public`.
- `phrasePresent` was first placed in `archiveVerification.ts`, which made the loader depend on the archive service and, through
  it, on `axios`; it moved to the pure `lib/htmlText.ts` beside the normalisation it rests on.
